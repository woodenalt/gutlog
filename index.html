Local data controls

Deleting data cannot be undone without a backup. No account or remote sync is included.

Privacy and reliability

IndexedDB stores entries locally; offline app files are cached by a service worker. There is no analytics, ad SDK, account, or network submission of health entries. This is not encryption: anyone with access to an unlocked device/browser profile may be able to read the records. iOS can erase site data, so export backups regularly. Hosting serves only app code, not your personal records.
